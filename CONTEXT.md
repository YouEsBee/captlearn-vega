# CaptLearn

A TV app where English speakers learn Mandarin by watching videos with Mandarin captions, after a short quiz on the video's words.

## Words and progress

**Learnt**:
A word whose most recent quiz answer was correct. It changes only through quiz answers.
_Avoid_: Known, mastered

**New word**:
A word the learner has never answered correctly in a quiz.
_Avoid_: Unseen word, unknown word

**Box**:
A word's place on its review schedule. Each box has a longer gap before the word is due again.
_Avoid_: Level, stage

**Due**:
A learnt or lapsed word whose review gap has passed.
_Avoid_: Overdue, expired

**Lapse**:
A wrong quiz answer on a due word, which makes it no longer learnt.
_Avoid_: Forget, reset

## Watching

**Blanked**:
A learnt word removed from the English caption line while the learner watches.
_Avoid_: Hidden, substituted

**Glossed**:
A word shown with its English meaning while the learner watches.
_Avoid_: Translated, annotated

## Videos

**Catalogue**:
The set of videos a learner can pick from on the home screen.
_Avoid_: Library, content

**Lexicon**:
The teachable words of one video, each with an English gloss and an HSK level.
_Avoid_: Vocabulary list, dictionary

## The loop

**Quiz**:
The questions before a video: up to 8 of its new words and up to 5 due words.
_Avoid_: Test, pre-test

**Wrong option**:
An incorrect answer offered in a quiz question, drawn from words of a similar HSK level.
_Avoid_: Distractor

**Summary**:
The screen after a video that shows what the quiz changed: newly learnt, reviewed and kept, and lapsed words.
_Avoid_: Results, report
